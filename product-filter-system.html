Our Product

Perfect Filter System

Whole House System

Description

A salt-free whole-house purification system built around our proprietary Cascadion Media, designed exclusively for our customers. It's engineered to reduce microplastics, PFAS and other "forever chemicals," heavy metals, pharmaceuticals, odor and stale taste — without stripping the water of what makes it good.

Rather than removing calcium and magnesium outright, the system reacts with them directly — inhibiting scale buildup on shower glass and fixtures while leaving those minerals in your water, where they belong for your health. A fine-micron filtering process means it only needs a 15–20 second rinse per week, a fraction of the 150+ gallons a traditional salt-based softener burns through backwashing every month.

Specifications

  • Stainless Steel Housing
  • 115 Volts
  • 20–25 GPM Flow
  • No Salt Required
  • Less Water Waste
