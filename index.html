Whole-Home Filtration, Reimagined

Water,
perfected.

We didn't set out to start another water company. We started because the water itself had changed, and almost no one was saying so out loud.

Working inside real homes, testing real water, one pattern was impossible to miss: the filtration technology most companies still rely on hasn't meaningfully changed in decades — while what's actually coming through the tap has.

Microplastics. PFAS. Trace pharmaceuticals. Threats that barely registered a generation ago are now turning up in water supplies across the country.

Plenty of companies can filter water. Very few are built around what's actually in it today.

We think trust starts with transparency, which is why every visit begins with a free in-home water test — not to alarm you, not to sell you, just to show you exactly what's there. Once you can see your own water clearly, the right decision tends to make itself.

Perfect Filter exists for a simple reason: your water should never be something you have to wonder about.

The Object

Four decisions,
held to one standard.

Filtration

A twelve-stage cascade, tuned to 0.01 microns.

Sediment, PFAS, heavy metals and microplastics are drawn out in sequence by our proprietary Cascadion media — not filtered away, refined out — leaving the mineral character of the water intact.

Materials

Brushed stainless, cast glass, no visible seams.

Every surface is chosen for how it ages — a matte, fingerprint-resistant finish that is machined, not moulded, and built to sit in view rather than be hidden under a sink.

Silence

18 decibels — quieter than a library.

The pump and valve assembly is isolated on a floating chassis, so filtration happens without a sound in the room it lives in.

Footprint

A single high-capacity cartridge replaces the plastic of roughly 1,800 bottles annually — recyclable, and delivered before you need to ask.

The Cascade

Twelve stages.
One uninterrupted pass.

Standard filtration was built for a different era's water problems — sediment, chlorine, hard minerals. It was never built for what's actually coming through the tap now: PFAS, microplastics, and trace pharmaceuticals that barely entered the conversation until the last few years. This cascade was engineered for that newer, harder problem.

Stages 1–3 · Pre-Filtration

Sediment cleared before it can reach the membrane.

Graduated mesh layers remove sand, rust and particulate — protecting every stage that follows and extending membrane life.

Stages 4–6 · Cascadion Media

PFAS, chlorine and odor — bound at the molecular level.

Our proprietary Cascadion media adsorbs "forever chemicals" and chemical taste alike, without stripping the water of character.

Stages 7–10 · RO Membrane

A 0.01 micron pass for dissolved solids and metals.

The reverse-osmosis membrane rejects lead, microplastics and dissolved salts most systems let through.

Stages 11–12 · Remineralization

Calcium and magnesium, reintroduced.

The final pass restores the minerals that give water its body — and brings pH back to a natural balance.
