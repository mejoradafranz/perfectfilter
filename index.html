The Cascade

Twelve stages.
One uninterrupted pass.

Standard filtration was built for a different era's water problems — sediment, chlorine, hard minerals. It was never built for what's actually coming through the tap now: PFAS, microplastics, and trace pharmaceuticals that barely entered the conversation until the last few years. This cascade was engineered for that newer, harder problem.

Stages 1–3 · Pre-Filtration

Sediment cleared before it can reach the membrane.

Graduated mesh layers remove sand, rust and particulate — protecting every stage that follows and extending membrane life.

Stages 4–6 · Cascadion Media

PFAS, chlorine and odor — bound at the molecular level.

Our proprietary Cascadion media adsorbs "forever chemicals" and chemical taste alike, without stripping the water of character.

Stages 7–10 · RO Membrane

A 0.01 micron pass for dissolved solids and metals.

The reverse-osmosis membrane rejects lead, microplastics and dissolved salts most systems let through.

Stages 11–12 · Remineralization

Calcium and magnesium, reintroduced.

The final pass restores the minerals that give water its body — and brings pH back to a natural balance.
