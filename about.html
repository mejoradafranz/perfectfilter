About Us

Built in the field,
not a boardroom.

Perfect Filter started in real homes, working through real water problems most companies were content to ignore.

Between us, we'd spent years inside the water industry — installing and servicing nearly every kind of system on the market: the genuinely good, the outdated, the overpriced, and more than a few plastic tanks dressed up in clever marketing. One thing became impossible to unsee: families needed clean water without salt, gimmicks, or complications, and almost nothing on the market actually delivered that.

The same problems kept turning up, home after home — chlorine, heavy metals, microplastics, scale buildup, strange tastes and smells, skin irritation, damaged plumbing, appliances failing years too early. Every so-called fix seemed to solve one problem while quietly creating another. Perfect Filter was built to end that cycle.

The Technology

Perfected once, engineered around ever since.

We built a filtration system capable of reaching down to 0.01 microns — fine enough to capture microplastics and fine particulates before they ever reach your taps. Paired with our proprietary Cascadion Media, the system also reduces heavy metals, pharmaceuticals, PFAS, forever chemicals, chlorine and chloramines.

Unlike a traditional softener, it protects your plumbing from scale buildup without stripping out the minerals your body actually needs — no salt, and none of the wasteful regeneration cycles that can burn through 150-plus gallons of water a month.

Once the technology was right, we engineered the rest of the system around it. The goal was simple: build the last home filtration system a family will ever need.

  • Stainless steel housings
  • 0.01 micron filtration
  • High flow for large homes
  • Low maintenance
  • Long service life

Our Belief

A name is either a promise or it's marketing.

The name "Perfect Filter" was never meant as a slogan. It's a commitment: build something we'd be proud to install in our own homes, stand behind it fully, and never cut corners to get there.

We believe clean water shouldn't be a luxury reserved for one kind of home. Whether it's a small apartment or a house on the hill, every family deserves water they don't have to think twice about.
